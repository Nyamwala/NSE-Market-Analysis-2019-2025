1. What is the problem you are solving?
   The Problem: Analyzing and investing in the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE) between 2019 and 2025 has been uniquely challenging due to severe macroeconomic volatility and structural market constraints. Investors and analysts have had to navigate major historical disruptions—including the economic fallout of the COVID-19 pandemic, aggressive monetary tightening, historic inflation spikes, and high-stakes political uncertainty surrounding the 2022 general elections. Chronic liquidity disparities where a handful of blue-chip counters dominate daily turnover while dozens of other stocks suffer from thin trading, sudden regulatory reforms, and shifting interest rate regimes following the repeal of the interest rate cap.
   The Impact: Without a rigorous, multi-year analytical framework, everyday investors, fund managers and retail traders struggle to separate true structural growth from false price movements caused by low liquidity. This leads to poor capital allocation, unexpected capital drawdowns during market crashes, and an inability to accurately measure how Kenyan equities withstand systemic political and economic shocks.

2. What tools did you use?
   Python & Pandas: Cleaned, structured, and merged over 121,000 daily trading records across 7 years of market data and official sector mapping files into unified datasets.
   Jupyter Notebooks: Used as the core interactive environment for running exploratory data analysis, writing calculation scripts, and computing financial KPIs.
   VS Code & Git: Used as the development environment for writing code, managing local folder paths, and running version control for your GitHub repository.
   Tableau / Data Visualization Libraries: Prepared for developing visual dashboards to display sector returns, price crashes, and trading volume trends.

3. What insights did you or do you want to discover? / What Solutions do you want to offer?

* Which business sectors (such as Banking, Telecommunications, or Manufacturing) generated the best steady returns relative to their risk from 2019 to 2025?

* How severely did systemic shocks like the COVID-19 crash, monetary policy shifts, and the 2022 general election cycle impact stock prices, and what were their recovery timelines?

* Which counters suffer from "thin-trading" risks where low daily volume creates false price movements?

* How do individual blue-chip stocks stack up against overall market benchmarks like the All-Share Index (^NASI)?

The Solutions I Offer:
Multi-Year Market Intelligence: Provided a clean, automated Python pipeline and structured datasets that track 7 years of daily NSE activity without manual spreadsheet errors.
Risk & Liquidity Guidance: Enabled everyday investors to distinguish safe, reliable dividend-paying blue-chip counters from risky, thinly-traded stocks before putting their capital at risk.

4. How would a business or a community benefit from your work?
   For Everyday Investors & Retail Traders: Replaces guesswork with clear, data-backed insights, helping individuals protect their savings, understand volatility, and pick stronger long-term stocks.
   For Financial Analysts & the Local Investment Community: Brings absolute transparency to the Kenyan capital markets, offering a reusable, open-source analytical framework that anyone can use to study stock resilience and market trends.
